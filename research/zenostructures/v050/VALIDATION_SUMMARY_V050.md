## Private tooling boundary

Private invention and verification systems were used or prepared during research. Their implementations, operator grammars, prompts, proposal frontiers, internal scoring policies, adapters, and detailed traces are intentionally omitted from this public repository.

The public receipt binds only the scoped inputs and bounds, result counts, opaque output digests, replay status, and explicit nonclaims. An opaque digest is not a public specification of the private tool and does not make the tool part of the ZenoFCIS trusted computing base.

## Prepared but not publicly exposed

Some proof, oracle, model, and cross-runtime work remained unexecuted in the construction environment. The private details and tool-specific inputs are retained outside the public repository. Their absence from the public branch is intentional, not an evidence claim.

## Nonclaims
