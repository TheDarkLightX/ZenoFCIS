ADR0004 contains no entry that promises the 12-type compound executor or
full-width U128 zUSD. Those are two explicitly moved profile obligations,
recorded in the V2.1 addendum; they are not invented as new ledger quotations.
The [ADR](adr/0004-v2-ledger.md) adopts these 13 withdrawal reasons. Rows 10,
17 and 25 retain their semantic route requirements through replacement APIs: V2
law predicates replace the retired law/guard spelling, native state-domain
callbacks are retired, and root/version/certificate freshness coexists with
the exact state bytes required for execution. Required entries remain open
until final qualification; code observations do not close release entries.

Do not withdraw the footprint, library-law, source/policy identity, root/CAS,
replay, certificate/chain or delivery-interpreter protections. The pair
export/import API is withdrawn by the approved S1 design, while different-policy
replay refusal and stored-genesis tamper refusal remain mandatory.

The baseline tree is a local recovery anchor, not yet a published remote artifact.
